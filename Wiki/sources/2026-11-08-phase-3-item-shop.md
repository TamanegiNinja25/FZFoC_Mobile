# Phase 3 — item shop with categories

A fountain shop with 13 original items in 5 categories (Weapons, Armor, Mobility, Relics, Consumables), 6 inventory slots, half-price selling, consumables tapped from the HUD, and bots that buy a fixed build. Verified by a 17-check test and on the phone.

## Key points
- **Rules:** shop at your own fountain; match keeps running; 6 slots; sell for 50%; stats recomputed from definition + items.
- **Catalogue:** Weapons (Tempered Blade, Quickening Gauntlets, Forgebreaker Maul), Armor (Riveted Mail, Bulwark Plate, Emberheart Amulet), Mobility (Wayfarer's Treads, Windstep Greaves), Relics (Kindling Rune, Everflame Sigil, Sage's Hourglass), Consumables (Ember Draught, Spark Flask); 100–900 gold.
- **Relics** tie items to the transformation meter — the game's own mechanic.
- **Bots** buy blade → mail → treads → rune → plate → maul at their fountain.
- **Originality:** names avoid Warcraft III items; icons generated from code.
- **Verification:** shop test 17/17; phone check of SHOP button and shop screen.
