# Decision — keep the transformed-hero bounty as risk/reward

The owner kept the higher bounty on transformed heroes, reframed from a comeback lever to risk/reward for the transformed player: stronger, but a richer target while the form lasts.

## Key points
- **Decision:** transformed heroes are worth more gold when killed — risk/reward, not a comeback mechanic.
- **Why it fits "no comeback":** it rewards whoever takes on the strongest enemy, rather than helping a losing team.
- **Prototype values (placeholders):** base hero bounty 300 gold; ×1.75 in form (525), the midpoint of the spec's 1.5–2×. The HUD shows the current bounty.
