# UI polish and Phase 2 — 3v3 arena against adaptive AI

Layout fixes, menu animation, original synthesised sounds and app icon, and a smoother Vael; then Phase 2: an original 3v3 arena against bots whose decisions — not stats — adapt to the score. Locked 60 FPS on the phone after fixing primitive detail and stale desktop settings. Reverse-engineering the map's models was declined as derivative copying.

## Key points
- **Models line:** reverse-engineering the map's models would still produce derivative copies; declined. Own models polished instead.
- **Polish:** safe-area insets, HUD backing panel, styled tabs, fixed button overlap; slide/pulse/pop-in animation and press feedback; 10 original sounds; original hammer-and-slash icon with Android adaptive layers; all generated by `tools/generate_assets.py`; Reset to Defaults.
- **Vael model:** rounded pauldrons, barrel hammer, round shield with team emblem, belt; top-visible glow on shoulder vents and helm crest.
- **Arena:** two bases with fountains (90 HP/s), central lane, rune circle, pillars, neutral camps; first to 15 kills or 8:00; scoreboard, kill feed, result screen.
- **Combat:** shared targeting for heroes and neutrals; killers earn the victim's bounty.
- **Adaptive AI:** reaction 0.7 → 0.15 s, retreat 0 → 38% HP, nearest vs weakest target, accuracy 30 → 95%, form timing, dash finishing; enemy skill 0.5 + 0.1 per kill of the player's lead (0.15–0.95); allies 0.55; stats unchanged.
- **Tests:** arena test 9/9; caught auto-attacks only hitting dummies; 7 suites, 0 script errors.
- **Performance:** primitives default to high detail with no LODs (890k → 50k triangles); the real slowdown was stale desktop settings on the phone; with phone defaults, locked 60 FPS at GPU 10.2 ms.
