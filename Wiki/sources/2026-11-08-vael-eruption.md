# Vael's Eruption — empowered skill design

Eruption is Bellows Slam while Kindled: radius ×1.5 (3.0 → 4.5) and damage ×1.2 before the form's ×1.25, so 180 effective damage — ×1.5 overall, at the top of the spec's form budget. Area was favoured, as in the reference map's empowered forms. Verified end to end in the prototype.

## Key points
- **Values:** radius 4.5; damage 144 base, 180 effective with Kindled.
- **Rationale:** area growth reads clearly on a phone and matches how the reference's empowered forms grew; total power stays within +30–50%.
- **Verification:** kit test 15/15; a dummy 4 units away takes 180 from Eruption and nothing from Bellows Slam.
- **Watch:** ×1.5 radius is ×2.25 area, so Eruption's crowd value grows more than its single-target value.
