# Phase 5 — heroes Ryn and Barrow, original music

Two original heroes complete the speed / bruiser / giant set: Ryn (Speed: Cyclone Cut, Flurry, Tempest) and Barrow (Giant: Quake Stomp, Boulder Hurl, Avalanche). New haste, slow and projectile systems; per-skill AI hints let bots play any hero. Original generated menu and battle music.

## Key points
- **Ryn:** 820 HP, move 7.0, 26 damage every 0.55 s; Cyclone Cut (dash slicing the path, 100, 7 s); Flurry (+60% attack speed for 4 s, 10 s); Tempest (12 s, ×1.4 speed) → Tempest Cut (9 units, 150).
- **Barrow:** 1,450 HP, move 5.0, 58 damage every 1.3 s; Quake Stomp (90 within 3.5 + 40% slow, 8 s); Boulder Hurl (projectile, 160, 9 s); Avalanche (14 s, ×1.7 size, 35% shield) → 4.9 radius, 55% slow.
- **Systems:** haste, slows, projectiles; skills give bots casting hints; shared model base; bots mix heroes by slot; lobby hero choice synced online.
- **Music:** menu theme and 110 BPM battle loop, generated from code.
- **Verified:** roster test 17/17; all 11 suites; phone at 60 FPS with mixed heroes.
