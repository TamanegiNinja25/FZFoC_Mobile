# Phase 7 — feel, eight heroes and the original's rules

Physical, animated creeps with map-style pile-ups; damage numbers; hero animation, effects and skill icons; five new heroes after the reference map's mechanic families; utility and exclusive items; Classic as the original's 4v4 with unique picks and the leaver rule; a simulation-based balance pass; joining by address; and phone stress testing that led to a round of performance work.

## Key points
- **Creeps:** physics bodies that block heroes; animated; camps grow to 60 by default; four camps moved away from the bases after piles reached the fountains in a phone test.
- **Heroes:** Sable (ranged), Morrow (summoner), Kael (transforms on his own below 10% HP, the map's mechanic), Tamsin (storm caster), Hask (guardian); realms group heroes like the map's taverns.
- **Items:** an exclusive item per hero; recall scroll, throwing knives, shadow cloak, capture orb (after the map's mixed shop); 45 items.
- **Classic:** 4v4, each hero once, the map's leaver gold (2,000 / 6,000 / 18,000 by level), up to 8 players.
- **Balance:** three rounds of 16 bot matches; seven of eight heroes at 33–62% win rate, Sable buffed after 18%.
- **Phone:** 46–51 FPS with 570 creeps in 4v4 Classic after the launch boost ends, matching the near-empty training ground (53 FPS) on the warm phone; 60 FPS while boosted.
