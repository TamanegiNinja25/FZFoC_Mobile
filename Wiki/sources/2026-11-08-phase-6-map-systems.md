# Phase 6 — the reference map's systems

The fidelity audit's six adoptions, built into the prototype (v0.6.0): levels 1–50 with attributes and ranked skills, the duel, creep waves at 12 camps, two boss chains, a 100,000-HP win boss, a stat shop, exclusive items and recipes, and Mobile / Classic modes. It also adds an attack button with the skills on an arc. The record lists the map facts each system copies.

## Key points
- **Map facts:** 100 team kills or the win boss ends a match; 24 heroes in four series taverns, picked once each by wisp; level 50 cap; 10 HP and 0.1 HP/s per strength, 2 damage per primary point, 0.5 armour per agility; creep XP reduction 80 / 70 / 60 / 50%; mana-free skills mostly scaling with attributes.
- **Duel:** every 290–310 s, world frozen, one random hero per team, 3-2-1 Fight, winner earns level × 200 gold.
- **Creep waves:** 42 every 30 s, 68 every 60 s, 49 every 90 s (only once the last 90 s wave is dead), with the map's per-type HP, bounty and level.
- **Bosses:** chains of 9 and 7 (500 → 12,500 HP), next boss 10 s after a kill.
- **Built:** XP curve and sharing (Mobile ×3), attributes counting only gains after level 1, four skills per hero (slot rules 1 / 2 / 5 / 10, skip 5), six new skills, ATTACK + arc + "+" badges, gold on the map's scale (×10), 7 tomes, 3 exclusive items, 7 recipes, Classic mode (50 kills, no limit), creep caps 10 / camp and 80 total.
- **Verified:** 52-check map systems test; pacing test (ultimates ~6 min, average level 17 at 8:00); all suites and the network test pass. Phone check pending.
