# UI and audio direction

The map's UI and audio can't be extracted — Blizzard owns the UI art, icons, voices and effects, and the imported clips are most likely from anime. The nostalgia lives in recognisable patterns that can be rebuilt with original or licensed assets. Includes a same-day sound-event analysis that corrects an earlier claim about the map's MP3s.

## Key points
- **Not usable:** UI frames, fonts, cursors, icons, voices and effects (Blizzard); the 60 imported clips (most likely anime recordings). The repo is public, so extraction would mean publication.
- **Usable:** the originals as mood reference. Style isn't protected; specific recordings, images and melodies are. Sound-alikes and traced icons still count as copies.
- **UI patterns:** framed bottom panel (portrait, ability grid, 6-slot inventory), bevelled stone or metal frames with gold trim, parchment tooltips, top resource bar, corner minimap. On mobile: corner portrait, skill-button arc by the right thumb, pull-up inventory.
- **Audio patterns:** select and order voice lines (the biggest nostalgia trigger), chunky UI sounds, orchestral and folk music with war drums, day/night ambience.
- **Sources:** Kenney, itch.io, OpenGameArt (UI); Google Fonts Cinzel, MedievalSharp, IM Fell English; Freesound, Sonniss, Kenney (SFX); OpenGameArt, Incompetech, Soundimage (music); game-icons.net (icons); own recordings or a voice actor (voices).
- **Correction:** the 60 imported MP3s are short clips of 0.2–6.1 s (median 0.8 s), not music tracks.
- **Sound events:** 45 abilities each have their own cast clip (29 global, 23 positional). Select and order voices are stock Warcraft III sets (22 heroes); music is the default playlist; ambience is a stock city loop.
- **For an original version:** about 46 sounds for a 3-hero prototype, mostly voice work. On phones, play your own team's casts in full and enemy casts quieter and positional.
