# Hero transformations

A mechanic where a hero changes into a stronger form mid-fight. It is the standout idea in [Fight Of Characters](fight-of-characters.md) and a strong candidate to carry into an original mobile game.

## In Fight Of Characters
- Transformations are implemented as separate hero units: 36 hero forms in total ([src](../sources/2026-11-08-fight-of-characters-mobile-feasibility.md)).
- Examples: Goku → Super Saiyan 1, 2 and 3; Ichigo → Bankai; Luffy → Gear ([src](../sources/2026-11-08-fight-of-characters-mobile-feasibility.md)).
- The source names mid-fight transformations as the map's standout idea and recommends keeping the mechanic ([src](../sources/2026-11-08-fight-of-characters-mobile-feasibility.md)).

## Design notes
- Swapping the whole unit is a workaround for Warcraft III's engine; a custom game could treat a form as a state on one character instead *(inference)*.
- The mechanic itself is free to reuse; the specific transformations shown above belong to their franchises — see [copyright vs game mechanics](copyright-vs-game-mechanics.md) ([src](../sources/2026-11-08-fight-of-characters-mobile-feasibility.md)).
- Open design questions *(inference)*: what triggers a transformation (meter, timer, kills), how long it lasts, and what it costs, so it creates comeback moments without deciding fights on its own.
